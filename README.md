# The Herdsman - Kraken Engine Fall Jam 2025

![The Herdsman and their sheep](./sheep.png)

Work in progress game, originally created for the Kraken Engine Fall Jame 2025.

Herd your sheep through mine-scarred pastures while unseen
Chupacabras stalk the hills. Scavenge supplies,
keep your flock calm, and fortify your ranch before night falls.
